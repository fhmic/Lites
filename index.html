LITE

Personal automation agent — scheduling, documents, Gmail drafting, and more.

What LITE does

LITE is a personal assistant application that runs on its owner's own computer and helps with day-to-day tasks:

  • Calendar management — creating and listing events on the owner's own Google Calendar, always with confirmation before any guest is invited.
  • Gmail inbox review — reading recent unread email and showing its content on screen. LITE can also write replies, but only as Gmail drafts: nothing is ever sent without the owner opening, reviewing, and sending it themselves. LITE has no ability to send email.
  • Document & presentation generation — creating .docx and .pptx files from a description or outline.
  • Reminders and scheduled inbox scans — OS-level reminders and an automated daily scan that flags email worth the owner's attention.
  • Research and web tasks — browsing public websites to research, compare, and summarize information.

Who LITE is for

LITE is a single-user, personal application. It is installed and used only by its developer and owner on their own machine, and it accesses only the owner's own Google account (Gmail and Calendar). It is not offered to the public as a service and does not have other users.
